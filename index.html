My Own Product

Orion Knowledge Hub

🔭
Orion Knowledge Hub
Fully AI-Based Knowledge Platform
✦ Founder & Creator 100% AI-Driven

Orion Knowledge Hub is a completely AI-based platform built from the ground up to extract, organise, and surface knowledge on demand. The core concept is to train on pre-loaded content and enable natural-language interaction with that knowledge — making complex information instantly accessible for both students and professionals.

🎓
For Students
Upload course materials, textbooks, or study notes. Orion trains on pre-loaded content and acts as an intelligent study companion — answering questions, summarising topics, and reinforcing learning through contextual knowledge extraction.
💼
For Professionals
Upload meeting transcripts and train Orion with that session's knowledge. It surfaces answers to any question about what was discussed, generates structured notes, and extracts key decisions and action items — turning hours of meetings into instant, searchable intelligence.
